# Skill 03 — Product Owner Skill (GBS-AIOS)

## Purpose
Manages feature scoping, prioritization, roadmap consistency, backwards compatibility, and feature flag toggles across all GBS portals.

## Responsibilities
• Enforce strict scope control; reject feature bloat and premature complexity.
• Ensure every new capability has feature-flag backing for zero-downtime rollouts.
• Maintain alignment with `docs/overview.md` and `docs/billing_subscriptions_and_support.md`.
• Validate that partner white-labeling capabilities remain preserved.
