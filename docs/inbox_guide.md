# Unified Inbox Guide — GBS Phase 19

The **Unified Inbox** aggregates customer communication across 10 official channels into a single enterprise-grade interface.

## Supported Channels
1. Google Business Profile Messages
2. Google Reviews & Replies
3. Facebook Messenger
4. Instagram Direct Messages
5. WhatsApp Business API
6. Email (SMTP / IMAP / SendGrid)
7. SMS (Twilio / AWS SNS)
8. Website Live Chat Widget
9. Telegram Bot API
10. Microsoft Teams Bot

## Core Features
- **Unified Timeline**: View all conversation messages, media previews, voice notes, attachments, and internal notes.
- **Smart Queues**: Categorized into Unread, Priority, Starred, Spam, and Custom Folders.
- **AI Suggested Replies & Summaries**: Automatic AI response drafting and one-click conversation summarization.
- **Conversation Translation**: Real-time multi-language translation for cross-border support.
- **Merge & Split**: Consolidate duplicate customer threads into a single timeline with full audit logging.
- **Spam & Sentiment Detection**: Automatic classification of positive, neutral, and negative sentiment.
