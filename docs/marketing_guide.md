# Global Business Suite (GBS) — Omnichannel Marketing Guide

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Target Roles**: Marketing Managers, Growth Engineers, Campaign Specialist, Partner Admins  

---

## 1. Overview & Capabilities

The GBS Marketing Center allows enterprise teams to create, automate, and track campaigns across multiple communications channels:

- **Channels**: Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push Notifications, Omnichannel.
- **A/B Testing**: Create subject line and content variants to optimize open and click rates.
- **Audience Segmentation**: Build dynamic rule-based audience segments.
- **Automation Journeys**: Multi-step visual automation nurture flows.
- **ROI Analytics**: Real-time conversion, open rate, click-through rate, and estimated revenue attribution.

---

## 2. Launching a Campaign

1. Navigate to **Marketing Center** in Admin or Partner Portal.
2. Click **Create Campaign**, select channel (Email / SMS / WhatsApp / Push), subject, and body text.
3. Add A/B testing variants if desired.
4. Select Target Audience Segment.
5. Click **Dispatch Campaign** to execute multi-channel delivery.
