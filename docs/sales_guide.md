# Global Business Suite (GBS) — Sales & Pipeline User Guide

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Target Roles**: Sales Representatives, Account Executives, Partner Sales Admins, Sales Managers  

---

## 1. Overview & Capabilities

The GBS Enterprise Sales Module enables end-to-end management of sales leads, deals, pipelines, activities, quotations, and contract execution.

- **Kanban Deal Pipelines**: Drag-and-drop opportunity cards across customizable pipeline stages.
- **AI Deal Intelligence**: Predictive win probability, deal health scoring, and AI recommendations.
- **Lead Conversion**: Convert captured leads into Accounts, Contacts, and Opportunities in a single click.
- **Digital Signatures**: Send legally compliant contracts and proposals with instant cryptographic verification.

---

## 2. Sales Workflow

### Step 1: Lead Capture & AI Qualification
- Leads enter via Website Forms, Landing Pages, Social Lead Ads, or Google Business Profile messages.
- AI automatically evaluates the lead profile and assigns a Lead Score (0-100).
- High-intent leads (Score ≥ 75) trigger immediate notification alerts.

### Step 2: Opportunity Management & Kanban Board
- Navigate to **Monetization & CRM -> CRM & Sales Pipelines**.
- View deals organized by stage (New Lead, Contacted, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Closed Won).
- Drag opportunity cards to advance stages. Deal probabilities update automatically.

### Step 3: Proposals & Digital Signature
- Navigate to **Documents Vault** to create or review proposals.
- Click **Digital Sign Document** to sign contracts with SHA-256 cryptographic audit logs.
