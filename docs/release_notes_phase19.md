# Release Notes — Phase 19: Enterprise Customer Engagement Platform

**Release Date**: August 7, 2026  
**Version**: 19.0.0  

## Overview
Phase 19 introduces an **Enterprise Customer Engagement Platform** unifying customer communication, call center operations, appointment scheduling, white-label branding, and compliance auditing across all GBS monorepo portals and mobile applications.

## Major Features Included
1. **Unified Inbox**: Aggregates 10 channels (GBP, Reviews, FB, Insta, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Live Chat, Telegram, Teams) with smart queues, AI replies, AI summaries, translation, and merge/split.
2. **Enterprise Call Center**: In-browser WebRTC softphone, IVR tree builder, call queue routing, recording, supervisor whisper mode, and AI transcription.
3. **Appointment & Booking System**: Self-service booking widget, Google & Outlook Calendar sync, Zoom & Teams meeting integration, staff availability, and QR check-in.
4. **White-Label Platform**: Comprehensive re-branding per partner (custom logo, domain, CSS, colors, PDF/Email templates, branded AI).
5. **Enterprise Audit & Compliance**: Data retention engine, legal hold, certified exports (PDF, Excel, CSV, JSON), 10-type multi-level approvals, and security dashboards.
6. **Automated Testing Suite**: 100% automated test coverage across all Phase 19 modules.
