# Enterprise Call Center Guide — GBS Phase 19

The **Enterprise Call Center** provides in-browser WebRTC softphone calling, SIP trunking, multi-level IVR routing, and AI voice intelligence.

## Core Capabilities
- **Browser & Mobile Calling**: Zero-plugin WebRTC softphone dialer for agents.
- **Interactive Voice Response (IVR)**: Graphical tree builder for automated caller greetings, press-1 routing, and after-hours voicemail.
- **Call Queuing**: Round-robin, skill-based, and priority call distribution.
- **Call Recording & Monitoring**: Real-time supervisor whisper mode, call listening, and conference calling.
- **AI Voice Intelligence**: Automated speech-to-text transcription, AI call summaries, sentiment scoring, and action item extraction.
- **Callback Scheduling**: Self-service and automated missed call callback scheduling.
