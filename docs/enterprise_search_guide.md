# Global Business Suite (GBS) — Enterprise Search Guide

## Overview

GBS Enterprise Search delivers unified, high-performance semantic search across all platform entities:
- Documents & Attachments (PDFs, DOCX, Spreadsheets)
- Customer & CRM Records (Leads, Deals, Accounts, Contacts)
- Partner Portal Data & Client Scopes
- Google Business Profile Locations, Ratings & Reviews
- Social Media Posts, Comments & Engagement Feed
- Analytics Reports & KPI Scorecards
- System Audit Logs & Operational Triggers

## Features

- **Document Intelligence OCR**: Built-in automated invoice extraction, contract analysis, and summary generation.
- **Role-Based Search Scoping**: Results are filtered automatically according to tenant boundaries and RBAC permissions.
- **Global Keyboard Shortcut**: `Ctrl+K` trigger across Admin, Partner, and Customer Portals.

## API Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/ecosystem/search/unified?q=Enterprise`
