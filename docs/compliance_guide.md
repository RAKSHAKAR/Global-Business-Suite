# Enterprise Audit & Compliance Guide — GBS Phase 19

The **Enterprise Audit & Compliance Engine** delivers strict security auditing, automated data retention, legal holds, certified exports, and multi-level approval workflows.

## Core Pillars
1. **Immutable Audit Trail**: Logs every user action, authentication attempt, permission modification, configuration change, and API/webhook event.
2. **Data Retention Engine**: Configurable retention periods by module, tenant, and country with automatic purging or archiving.
3. **Legal Hold Enforcement**: Suspend automatic data cleanup for specified tenants or records subject to regulatory investigation.
4. **Compliance Export Suite**: Certified export bundle generator producing audit records in PDF, Excel (.xlsx), CSV, and JSON formats.
5. **10-Type Multi-Level Approvals**: Configurable multi-step approval workflows covering Marketing, Publishing, Billing, Refunds, Partners, Customers, Users, Documents, Workflows, and AI Actions.
6. **Security & Risk Dashboards**: Real-time compliance health score, failed login analytics, threat detection logs, and risk analysis metrics.
