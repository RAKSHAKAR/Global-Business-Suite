# Global Business Suite (GBS) — Integration Hub Guide

## Overview

The GBS Integration Hub connects GBS microservices with external enterprise platforms across productivity, CRM, ERP, accounting, payments, and business intelligence.

## Pre-Built Enterprise Connectors

- **Productivity & Communication**: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom
- **CRM & Marketing**: Salesforce Cloud, HubSpot, Zoho CRM
- **Finance & ERP**: QuickBooks Online, Xero, SAP S/4HANA, Oracle ERP Cloud
- **E-Commerce & Payments**: Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Stripe
- **BI & Analytics**: Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Grafana

## Connector Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/ecosystem/integrations/hub`
