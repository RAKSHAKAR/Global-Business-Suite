# White-Label Platform Guide — GBS Phase 19

The **White-Label Platform** empowers partners to fully rebrand Global Business Suite for their end clients under custom domains.

## Customization Capabilities
- **Brand Identity**: Custom App Title, Logo, Favicon, Brand Name, and Typography.
- **Custom Themes**: Tailored primary/secondary color palettes, custom CSS overrides, and dark/light modes.
- **Custom Domains**: Automatic SSL provisioning for CNAME custom partner domains (e.g., `portal.partner.com`).
- **Template Customization**: Branded PDF invoice templates, email headers/footers, and notification subject lines.
- **Branded AI Persona**: Customize the name, system prompt, and avatar of the built-in AI Assistant per tenant.
- **Tenant Isolation**: Complete data, billing, subscription, and feature flag scoping per white-label partner.
