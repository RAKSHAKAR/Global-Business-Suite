# GBS Enterprise Partner Portal Master Guide

## Overview

The **Enterprise Partner Portal** is the central AI-powered business management workspace for agency partners, business managers, and franchise operators on the Global Business Suite (GBS) platform.

---

## Key Feature Modules

### 1. Configurable Partner Dashboard
- **16 Standard Widgets**: Business Overview, GBP Sync, Social Media Suite, Reviews Rating, AI Usage, Leads, Customers, Tasks, Calendar, Revenue, Subscriptions, Notifications, Campaigns, Performance Telemetry, Reports, Pending Approvals.
- **Dynamic Controls**: Drag-and-drop ordering, saved dashboard layout presets per user, role-based visibility, real-time metric stream, and CSV export.

### 2. Business Profile & Multi-Branch
- Complete company information, multiple branch locations, working hours calendar, business categories, service areas, branding within admin boundaries, and document vault.

### 3. Team Management & Roles
- Employee lifecycle: Invite, suspend, activate, remove, role permission assignment, and bulk CSV import/export.

### 4. Customer & Lead CRM
- Client directory, lead prospects, tags, customer groups, interaction timeline, and stage tracking.

### 5. Google Business Profile (GBP 14+ Subsystem)
- Multi-location synchronization, verification status, GBP posts, reviews with AI reply generator, products catalog, services list, media gallery, Q&A manager, performance insights, messaging, bulk ops, and scheduling.

### 6. Omnichannel Social Media Suite
- 8 Platform Integrations: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Threads, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest.
- OAuth account connection, multi-channel post scheduler, AI content & image generation, campaign calendar, and platform analytics.

### 7. AI Assistant Studio
- 8 Specialized AI Tool Engines: Content Generator, Image Generator, Marketing Assistant, Business Advisor, SEO Assistant, Review Assistant, Campaign Assistant, Analytics Assistant.
- Prompt library and AI generation audit history.

### 8. Omnichannel Marketing & Automation
- Email, SMS, WhatsApp, and Push notification campaign builder, audience segmentation, automation rules, and scheduled broadcasts.

### 9. Sales CRM & Deals Pipeline
- Drag-and-drop Kanban deal board, lead tracking, activity logs, and follow-up reminders.

### 10. Calendar & Tasks
- Unified team calendar, appointments, meeting scheduler, task board, priorities, reminders, and recurring events.

### 11. Media Asset Vault (Files)
- Image/video gallery, document repository, marketing assets, and AI-generated files storage.

### 12. Enterprise Reports & Analytics
- 11 Report Suites: Business Performance, Revenue, Leads, Customers, GBP, Social, AI Usage, Marketing, Reviews, SEO, Team Productivity.
- Filtering, drill-down tables, PDF/CSV export, and scheduled email report rules.

### 13. Notification Control Center
- Multi-channel delivery preferences (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push, In-App).

### 14. Settings & Subscription Control
- Account profile, white-label branding, TOTP MFA security, API tokens, subscription plan overview, upgrades/downgrades, add-ons, invoices, and receipts.

### 15. Enterprise Global Command Search
- Keyboard shortcut `Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K` global search across Customers, Leads, Reviews, GBP, Social Posts, Reports, Files, Tasks, and Settings.

### 16. Partner Mobile App Parity
- Expo / React Native mobile app matching Partner Portal features, featuring offline mode handling, push notifications, biometric login toggle, deep links, camera uploads, and file sharing.
