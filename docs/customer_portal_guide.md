# GBS Enterprise Customer Portal Master Guide

## Overview

The **Enterprise Customer Portal** is the central self-service workspace empowering business customers to manage their complete relationship with a business—including services, orders, appointments, payments, documents, reviews, support, and messages—while respecting Admin Portal governance, RBAC limits, and multi-tenant security boundaries.

---

## Core Feature Modules

### 1. Configurable Customer Dashboard
- **18 Standard Widgets**: Business Overview (Marketing Score), Active Services, Orders, Appointments, Invoices, Payments, Quotations, Tickets, Tasks, Notifications, Loyalty Points, Reviews, Messages, Google Business Activity, Offers, Documents Vault, Reports, AI Assistant.
- **Dynamic Controls**: Drag-and-drop widget layout, saved layout presets per user, real-time metric streams, and CSV export.

### 2. Customer Profile & Security Governance
- Personal & business details, primary/billing addresses, contacts, preferences, language/timezone selection, profile/logo upload, MFA TOTP security setup, login history, connected devices, and privacy controls.

### 3. Subscribed Services Management
- Subscribed & purchased service catalog, renewal tracking, auto-renewal controls, service change requests (upgrades, downgrades, cancellations), and recommended add-ons.

### 4. Orders & Tracking Subsystem
- Order history, status tracking, itemized breakdown, shipment tracking ID, order notes, and invoice downloads.

### 5. Appointments & Virtual Meeting Booking
- Interactive availability calendar, appointment booking, rescheduling, cancellation, meeting video links, and appointment history.

### 6. Google Business Profile Customer Actions
- Submit customer reviews, edit reviews (where permitted), upload photos, view business updates, browse products/services/FAQs, and preview AI-suggested responses.

### 7. Omnichannel Customer Messaging
- Live chat, Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push notifications, internal support conversations, and real-time chat log.

### 8. Support Center & Knowledge Base
- Support tickets tracking, ticket creation, live chat, knowledge base articles, FAQs, and ticket escalation.

### 9. Specialized Customer AI Assistant Studio
- **7 Customer AI Tool Engines**:
  1. AI Help Assistant
  2. AI Knowledge Search
  3. AI FAQ Assistant
  4. AI Document Search
  5. AI Recommendation Engine
  6. AI Appointment Assistant
  7. AI Support Assistant
- AI Voice interface (`Web Speech API`) and command runner.

### 10. Document Vault
- Storage for contracts, agreements, invoices, receipts, reports, uploaded documents, and shared files.

### 11. Payments & Auto-Billing
- Invoice payment processing, online card payments, auto-pay method management, billing history, and payment receipts.

### 12. Customer Reports & Analytics
- 7 Report Domains: Orders, Payments, Appointments, Support, Business Activity, GBP Activity, Usage Statistics with PDF/CSV export.

### 13. Notification Control Center
- Multi-channel delivery preferences (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push, In-App) and event subscription toggles.

### 14. Enterprise Command Palette Search
- Keyboard shortcut `Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K` global search across Orders, Invoices, Appointments, Documents, Support, Messages, Reports, and Services.

### 15. Customer Mobile App Parity
- Expo / React Native mobile app matching Customer Portal features, featuring biometric login (FaceID), offline cache mode status, push notifications, deep links, QR code support, camera document upload, and location services.
