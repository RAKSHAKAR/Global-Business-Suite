# Global Business Suite (GBS) — Customer Success & Health Guide

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Target Roles**: Customer Success Managers, Account Managers, Support Admins  

---

## 1. Overview & Capabilities

The GBS Customer Success Platform safeguards customer retention and drives expansion MRR:

- **Customer Health Scores**: Composite 0-100 score calculated from NPS, CSAT, usage activity, support tickets, and billing status.
- **AI Churn Risk Prediction**: Machine learning algorithms flag at-risk accounts before churn occurs.
- **Onboarding & Success Plans**: Goal plans with trackable milestones.
- **Feedback Loop**: Integrated NPS and CSAT customer feedback collection.
- **Expansion & Renewal Pipeline**: Renewal tracking and upsell opportunities.

---

## 2. Managing Account Health

1. Access **Customer Success** from Admin Portal.
2. Review the **Account Health Matrix** (Healthy, Neutral, At Risk, Critical).
3. Inspect AI Churn Risk insights and recommended interventions.
4. Update Onboarding Milestones as client progresses through adoption phases.
