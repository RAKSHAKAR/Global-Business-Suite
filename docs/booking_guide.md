# Appointment & Booking System Guide — GBS Phase 19

The **Appointment & Booking System** enables online scheduling, staff calendar management, external calendar sync, and video consultations.

## Core Capabilities
- **Embeddable Booking Widget**: Customizable online widget with theme colors, buffer times, and service selection.
- **Calendar Synchronization**: Real-time two-way synchronization with Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook.
- **Video Consultation Integration**: Automatic Zoom and Microsoft Teams room link generation upon booking confirmation.
- **Staff & Location Availability Rules**: Granular weekly schedules, holiday calendars, break times, and multi-staff routing.
- **QR Code Check-In**: Instant self-service check-in at physical locations via unique QR codes.
- **Automated Notifications**: Email and SMS booking confirmations, reminders, and self-service reschedule/cancel links.
